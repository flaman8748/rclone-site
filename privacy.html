Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 12, 2026

Rclone Transmission is a personal application used by its owner to access, upload, download, and manage files in their own Google Drive account.

Data Access and Use

The application accesses Google Drive files only to perform operations requested by the account owner.

Data Sharing

The application does not sell or share Google user data with third parties. File transfers are performed using rclone.

Data Storage and Security

OAuth credentials are stored locally in the user's rclone configuration. Google Drive files are not stored on this website.

Revoking Access

Users can revoke access at any time through their Google Account security settings.
